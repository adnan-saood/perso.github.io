Our paper **"Two Proposed Indoor Multi-Cameras Positioning Systems Compared to Classical Geometry System"** has been accepted at the IEEE International Conference on Advanced Science and Sustainable Development Applications (ICASSDA 2018).

This research presents innovative approaches to indoor positioning using multi-camera systems and compares them to classical geometry-based methods.

The paper received the **Best Paper Award** at ICASSDA 2018, recognizing its contribution to the field of indoor localization and computer vision.
