As I conclude my position as Research Engineer in Biomedical Engineering at ICube Strasbourg, I reflect on an incredibly enriching experience.

I have been fortunate to work under the mentorship of Dr. Jonathan Vappou, whose guidance has been transformative. He redefined the role of a supervisor for me, striking the perfect balance between providing insights and fostering my growth in research.

I also extend my gratitude to Dr. Florent Nageotte for his meticulous insights and open-minded approach, accompanying me from my internship through my research engineer position.

The year and a half at ICube will forever be cherished as a foundational stepping stone for my future endeavors. Special thanks to everyone in the Robotics and Data Science for Healthcare (RDH) team for creating such a supportive and collaborative environment.

As one chapter closes, a new one begins with my upcoming PhD at ENSTA Paris.
