I am thrilled to announce that I have started my PhD thesis at the Systems Engineering and Computer Science Laboratory (U2IS) at École Nationale Supérieure de Techniques Avancées (ENSTA), part of Institut Polytechnique de Paris.

My thesis is funded by the IP Paris doctoral school, and I will be working on touch for robotics, haptics, tactility, and human-robot interaction under the supervision of Prof. Adriana Tapus.

I am extremely grateful for the opportunity to pursue this research and excited about the journey ahead. Throughout my academic path, I have been fortunate to encounter the right people at the right times, and I look forward to the new experiences and challenges that this PhD will bring.
