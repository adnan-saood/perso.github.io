I am happy to share that I have started my position as Research Engineer at ICube Strasbourg.

This position is a continuation of my work during my master's study in the HealthTech M2 program at the University of Strasbourg.

I will be working on optimization-based coverage path planning and robotic assistance for medical procedures, building on my Master's thesis work on Blood-Brain Barrier opening using focused ultrasound.

Looking forward to contributing to cutting-edge research in medical robotics!
