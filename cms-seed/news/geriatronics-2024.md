Thrilled to announce that our team won first place at the 4th Geriatronics Summer School organized by Friends of Geriatronics!

Our winning project, **PETRA: Personalized Enhancement of physioTherapy with Robotic Assistance**, addresses key challenges in robotic-assisted rehabilitation.

The 3-day event featured insightful lectures from pioneers in robotics and human-robot interaction, along with hands-on demos and team challenges.

Huge thanks to our coaches: Franziska Riegel, David Beckwée, Prof. Dana Kulic, Dr. Joachim Hermsdörfer, and Akhil Gopinath.

It was a pleasure working with my talented teammates: Kai Wu, Mohammad Khoshkdahan, Simone Nertinger, Muhammed Samir Öztürk, and Alexander Aasmann.
