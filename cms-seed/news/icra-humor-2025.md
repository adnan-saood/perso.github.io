Our paper **"Oh! It's Fun Chatting with You!" A Humor-aware Social Robot Chat Framework** has been accepted at the IEEE-RAS International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA 2025).

This work introduces an innovative framework for social robots that can engage in humor-aware conversations, enhancing the naturalness and engagement of human-robot interactions.

Co-authored with Zhang, H., García Cárdenas, J.J., Hei, X., and Tapus, A.

ICRA is one of the premier conferences in robotics, and we are excited to share this work with the international robotics community.
