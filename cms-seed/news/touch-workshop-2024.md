Successfully organized and hosted the workshop **"Beyond Words: The Role of Touch in Social Robots"** at ICSR 2024 in Naples, Italy.

The workshop brought together leading researchers to investigate how touch can shape and enrich human-robot interaction across diverse contexts.

We were honored to feature outstanding speakers including:
- Prof. Katherine J. Kuchenbecker (Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems)
- Dr. Morten Roed Frederiksen (ITU Copenhagen)
- Dr. Zhegong Shangguan (former PhD student from our lab)

The event fostered engaging discussions on the future of touch in social robotics and strengthened our research community.

Workshop organizers: Prof. Adriana Tapus, Prof. Patrick Hénaff, and myself.

Touch is more than a sense—it's a pathway to connection.
