Excited to share that my research article on COVID-19 lung CT image segmentation has topped the trending articles list in Springer Nature's BMC Medical Imaging.

The paper, **"COVID-19 lung CT image segmentation using deep learning methods: U-Net versus SegNet"**, investigates and compares deep learning techniques for semantic segmentation of COVID-19 infected tissue in CT lung images.

With over 8,000 accesses and multiple citations within months of publication, this work represents a strong foundation for my academic career.

Many thanks to Dr. Iyad Hatem for his supervision and for the image processing and computer vision course that inspired this research.
