Our paper **"Designing a Haptic Interface for Enhanced Non-Verbal Human-Robot Interaction: Integrating Heart and Lung Emotional Feedback"** has been accepted at the IEEE-RAS International Conference on Humanoid Robots (Humanoids 2024).

This research focuses on creating a haptic interface for humanoid robots to convey emotional feedback through heart and lung simulations, enhancing non-verbal interaction and emotional connection between humans and robots.

Co-authored with Liu, Y., and Tapus, A.

The work demonstrates how physical embodiment and haptic feedback can create more empathetic and emotionally aware robotic systems.
