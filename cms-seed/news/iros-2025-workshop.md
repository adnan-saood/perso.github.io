Excited to announce our upcoming workshop at IROS 2025 in Hangzhou, China!

**Workshop on Enhancing Human Engagement in Social Assistive Robotics (SAR)**  
Date: October 20, 2025

As Socially Assistive Robotics continues to evolve, adaptive interaction, cognitive load management, and long-term user engagement remain core challenges. This interdisciplinary workshop brings together voices from robotics, AI, cognitive science, and HCI to reimagine how assistive robots can better respond to human needs in healthcare, education, eldercare, and beyond.

#### Keynote Speakers
- Prof. Gentiane Venture
- Prof. Weidong Chen
- Prof. Xiaojuan Ma
- Prof. Geng Yang
- Prof. Barbara Bruno

**Submission Deadline**: August 3, 2025

Workshop organizers: Prof. Adriana Tapus, Dr. Carlos Cifuentes, and Xiaoxuan Hei.

[Workshop Website](https://iros2025-sar-workshop.github.io/)
